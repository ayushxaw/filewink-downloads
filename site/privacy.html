Privacy

Updated 27 November 2026.

Files and settings

Filewink processes the files you select on your computer. It saves outputs to the locations you choose and keeps preferences such as your output folder on your device. Filewink has no app account, uploads, or telemetry.

If you enable Explorer gestures, the helper checks the selected file when you use a Filewink shortcut and passes its path to the local app.

Downloads and support

The app can open its download page, a dependency download, or another desktop app when you choose an action. Those services and apps handle data according to their own privacy policies.

GitHub hosts this website and its downloads. GitHub may record IP addresses for security. GitHub’s privacy statement

Bug reports on GitHub are public. Include a description of the problem and leave personal files and identifying information out of the report.

Questions

Filewink is published by Ayush Awasthi. You can ask about this policy through Filewink support.
